PHAN TICH HE THONG SMARTLOCK (models.py) - SAU KHI GO BO 2FA
================================================================

1. TONG QUAN
----------------
Day la mo hinh du lieu cho he thong khoa cua thong minh (IoT smart lock),
gom 5 nhom chinh, dung Django ORM + Postgres (Supabase). Auth dung custom
User model (UUID pk, dang nhap bang email), ma hoa Fernet cho du lieu nhay cam.

2. NHOM MODEL
----------------
NHOM A - NGUOI DUNG & XAC THUC
  - User: custom user (AbstractBaseUser + PermissionsMixin), email lam
    USERNAME_FIELD, co is_active/email_verified rieng.
  - EmailOtpChallenge: OTP qua email, con lai 5 muc dich:
    EMAIL_VERIFY, SUPPORT_AUTH, PASSWORD_RESET, RECOVERY_CONFIRM, UPDATE_INFO.
    (Da bo purpose LOGIN_2FA - xem muc 3)
  - PendingRegistration: luu tam thong tin dang ky cho toi khi xac thuc OTP.
  - LoginOtpAttempt: rate-limit so lan gui OTP / reset password theo IP.
  - SystemSettings: cau hinh he thong (bat/tat dang ky, thoi han OTP, whitelist IP...).
  - Announcement: thong bao he thong cho toan bo user.

NHOM B - THIET BI
  - Device: khoa vat ly/simulated, gan owner, trang thai online/offline/maintenance.
  - DeviceStatusLog: log pin, tin hieu, trang thai khoa, canh bao tamper.
  - DeviceCommand: lenh gui xuong thiet bi (UNLOCK/LOCK/ADD_CARD/OTA...), co token hash.

NHOM C - QUYEN & CHIA SE
  - Permission: danh muc quyen (co co nhay cam is_sensitive).
  - DeviceAccess: chia se quyen truy cap thiet bi cho user khac, co han su dung.
  - AccessCard: the NFC gan voi user.
  - CardDeviceAccess: the nao dung duoc voi thiet bi nao.
  - DoorPinCode: ma PIN mo cua, ho tro dung 1 lan.

NHOM D - NFC READER
  - NfcReader / NfcReaderConfig: dau doc NFC va cau hinh tu dong.
  - NfcSession: phien quet the.
  - NfcLog: log su kien quet the (thanh cong/that bai/mat ket noi...).

NHOM E - HO TRO & NHAT KY
  - SupportRequest: yeu cau ho tro dac biet (reset remote, recovery, doi chu so
    huu...), bat buoc co recovery_code_hash voi cac hanh dong nhay cam.
  - Notification: thong bao rieng cho tung user.
  - AuditLog: nhat ky hanh dong toan he thong.

3. GO BO TINH NANG 2FA - DA THUC HIEN
----------------------------------------
2FA (xac thuc 2 buoc khi dang nhap) truoc day duoc cai bang 4 thanh phan.
Da xoa toan bo khoi models.py:

  a) EmailOtpChallenge.purpose = 'LOGIN_2FA'
     -> XOA choice nay. Cac purpose con lai (verify email, reset password,
        support auth...) khong phai 2FA nen giu nguyen.

  b) Model AccountBackupCode (ma du phong khoi phuc 2FA) -> XOA CA MODEL.

  c) Model TrustedLoginDevice ("thiet bi tin cay" de bo qua buoc OTP lan
     dang nhap sau) -> XOA CA MODEL. Model nay chi co y nghia khi co 2FA.

  d) Model RemoteAuthRequest (duyet dang nhap tu thiet bi khac - kieu push
     approval) -> XOA CA MODEL.

  Sau khi xoa: 3 class + 1 choice, tong 443 dong con lai (tu 478 dong).
  Khong con model/field nao tham chieu toi 3 class da xoa (da grep kiem tra).

  LUU Y: sau khi sua models.py, PHAI chay lai:
     python manage.py makemigrations smartlock
     (Django se tu tao migration DROP 3 bang tren + ALTER choices field)

4. NGUYEN NHAN LOI "relation smartlock_user does not exist"
----------------------------------------------------------------
Log lenh migrate cho thay:
   "Operations to perform: Apply all migrations: admin, auth, contenttypes, sessions"
   -> KHONG CO "smartlock" TRONG DANH SACH NAY.

Trong khi AUTH_USER_MODEL = 'smartlock.User', nen migration admin.0001_initial
can bang smartlock_user ton tai truoc (FK swappable dependency). Django bao
"Apply all migrations" khong liet ke smartlock nghia la Django nghi migration
cua app smartlock DA duoc apply roi (co dong trong bang django_migrations),
nhung bang smartlock_user THUC TE khong ton tai trong database Supabase nay.

=> Ket luan: bang django_migrations dang co du lieu "rac" (stale) - ghi nhan
   sai la smartlock da migrate xong, trong khi DB that (Supabase) chua tung
   co bang nay (co the do: migrate tung chay nham vao DB/schema khac, hoac
   bang bi xoa thu cong tren Supabase dashboard nhung khong xoa dong tuong
   ung trong django_migrations).

5. CACH SUA LOI MIGRATE (chon 1 trong 2, tuy con du lieu can giu khong)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
CACH A - Neu DB con moi / chua co du lieu that (khuyen nghi cho de tai):
  1. Vao Supabase SQL editor, chay:
       DROP SCHEMA public CASCADE;
       CREATE SCHEMA public;
  2. Chay lai tu dau:
       python manage.py makemigrations
       python manage.py migrate

CACH B - Neu muon giu du lieu cac app khac, chi reset rieng smartlock:
  1. Vao Supabase SQL editor, chay:
       DELETE FROM django_migrations WHERE app = 'smartlock';
  2. Kiem tra xem bang smartlock_user (va cac bang smartlock_* khac) co
     thuc su ton tai khong:
       SELECT table_name FROM information_schema.tables
       WHERE table_name LIKE 'smartlock_%';
     - Neu KHONG co bang nao -> chay: python manage.py migrate smartlock
     - Neu CO bang roi (chi la django_migrations bi mat dong ghi nhan)
       -> chay: python manage.py migrate smartlock --fake-initial
  3. Sau do chay tiep: python manage.py migrate

Sau khi 2 buoc tren xong, nho makemigrations lai truoc do (muc 3) de
migration phan anh dung models.py da bo 2FA, roi moi migrate.
