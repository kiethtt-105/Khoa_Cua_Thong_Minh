PHAN TICH CHUYEN SAU NGHIEP VU - HE THONG SMARTLOCK


--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DANG KY TAI KHOAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Input nguoi dung: email, username, mat khau, ho ten.

Buoc xu ly:
  1. Validate: email dung dinh dang, username hop le (do dai, ky tu cho
     phep), mat khau du manh.
  2. Goi User.objects.create_user(email, username, password, ...):
     - UserManager.create_user() chuan hoa email (lowercase), chuan hoa
       username (lowercase), hash mat khau (set_password), goi
       full_clean() -> User.clean() se kiem tra CHEO: username khong
       duoc trung voi email cua nguoi khac va nguoc lai.
     - Neu trung -> ValidationError -> tra loi 400 "Username/email da
       duoc su dung".
     - Neu OK -> INSERT vao bang "smartlock_user" (is_active=False,
       email_verified=False).
     - Signal post_save(User) tu dong INSERT 2 dong vao LoginIdentifier
       (kind=EMAIL, kind=USERNAME). Neu do dong thoi 2 user submit trung
       gia tri -> UniqueConstraint tren LoginIdentifier.value chan o
       muc DB (bao loi IntegrityError -> serializer bat va tra 400).
  3. Tao EmailVerificationToken (purpose=EMAIL_VERIFY):
     - Sinh token ngau nhien (vi du secrets.token_urlsafe(32)).
     - Luu token_hash = sha256(token) vao DB, KHONG luu token goc.
     - expires_at = now + SystemSettings.verification_token_expiry_minutes.
     - Gui email chua link: https://.../verify?token=<token_goc>&uid=<user_id>
  4. Ket qua tra ve nguoi dung: 201 Created, "Vui long kiem tra email de
     xac minh tai khoan."

Loi co the gap:
  - 400: email/username da ton tai (unique tren User hoac LoginIdentifier).
  - 400: mat khau khong du manh (AUTH_PASSWORD_VALIDATORS).
  - 403: SystemSettings.registration_enabled = False -> tu choi dang ky.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. XAC MINH EMAIL (bam link trong mail)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Input: token (tu URL), user_id.

Buoc xu ly:
  1. Tim EmailVerificationToken theo user_id + purpose=EMAIL_VERIFY +
     is_used=False + expires_at > now.
  2. So sanh sha256(token) voi token_hash trong DB.
  3. Neu khop:
     - UPDATE User: email_verified=True, is_active=True.
     - UPDATE EmailVerificationToken: is_used=True, used_at=now.
     - Ghi AuditLog (action='EMAIL_VERIFIED', actor_user=user).
  4. Ket qua: 200 OK, chuyen huong ve trang dang nhap.

Loi:
  - 400: token sai / da dung (is_used=True) / het han (expires_at < now)
    -> tra ve "Link xac minh khong hop le hoac da het han", goi y gui lai.
  - 404: khong tim thay user_id.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DANG NHAP (email HOAC username)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Input: identifier (email hoac username, nguoi dung go nguyen van), password.

Buoc xu ly:
  1. Kiem tra LoginLockout cua user tuong ung (neu tim duoc user theo
     identifier): neu locked_until > now -> TU CHOI NGAY, khong check
     password (tranh brute-force khi dang bi khoa), tra 423 Locked kem
     so phut con lai.
  2. Tra cuu User: thu WHERE email=identifier truoc, khong co thi thu
     WHERE username=identifier (o tang authentication backend, khong
     phai trong models.py). Vi email/username la duy nhat toan he thong
     (nho LoginIdentifier) nen ket qua luon ro rang, khong bao gio ra
     2 user cho 1 identifier.
  3. Ghi LoginAttemptLog (identifier, ip_address, user_agent, success=?).
  4a. Neu SAI mat khau hoac khong tim thay user:
      - Neu tim duoc user: UPDATE/CREATE LoginLockout cho user do:
          failed_attempts += 1
          Neu failed_attempts vuot nguong cho phep o stage hien tai ->
          stage += 1, locked_until = now + stage_minutes[stage]
          (stage_minutes lay tu SystemSettings.login_lockout_stage_minutes,
           mac dinh [5, 10, 30]; tu stage 3 tro di giu nguyen 30 phut).
          Neu vua chuyen sang stage moi:
            - Gui email canh bao dang nhap sai nhieu lan (dung
              EmailVerificationToken? KHONG - day chi la email thong bao,
              khong can token, gui thang qua EMAIL_BACKEND).
            - Tao Notification (user=user, type='SECURITY_LOCKOUT',
              severity='warning', title='Canh bao dang nhap',
              message='Tai khoan bi khoa X phut do dang nhap sai nhieu
              lan') -> frontend web/app poll/subscribe bang nay de hien
              push/toast canh bao.
            - warning_sent_at = now.
      - Tra 401 "Sai email/username hoac mat khau".
  4b. Neu DUNG mat khau:
      - Neu User.is_active=False (chua verify email) -> 403 "Vui long
        xac minh email truoc khi dang nhap".
      - RESET LoginLockout ve stage=0, failed_attempts=0, locked_until=NULL.
      - Tao session/JWT, tra 200 kem thong tin user.

Loi:
  - 423 Locked: dang trong thoi gian khoa (tra kem so giay/phut con lai).
  - 401: sai thong tin dang nhap.
  - 403: tai khoan chua active/chua verify email.

Cac bang lien quan: User, LoginIdentifier (tra cuu), LoginLockout (trang
thai khoa), LoginAttemptLog (audit + tinh brute-force theo IP),
Notification (canh bao), AuditLog (log hanh vi bao mat cap he thong).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. QUEN MAT KHAU
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. User nhap email -> tao EmailVerificationToken (purpose=PASSWORD_RESET),
     gui link qua mail (giong muc 2).
  2. User bam link, nhap mat khau moi -> BE verify token (giong buoc 2),
     neu hop le: set_password(mat_khau_moi), is_used=True cho token,
     RESET LoginLockout ve 0 (phong truong hop dang bi khoa do doan
     truoc quen mat khau roi thu sai lien tuc), ghi AuditLog.
  3. Ket qua: 200, yeu cau dang nhap lai.

Loi: token sai/het han -> 400. Email khong ton tai -> VAN tra ve 200
chung chung ("Neu email ton tai, ban se nhan duoc huong dan") de tranh
lo thong tin user nao ton tai trong he thong (chong do tim tai khoan).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THEM KHOA (THIET BI) VAO HE THONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------
KHONG dung OTP. Dung co che "ma vat ly cua thiet bi" co san trong Device.

Buoc xu ly (nguoi dung la chu so huu moi cua khoa):
  1. Thiet bi khi xuat xuong da duoc set san: device_code (in/dan tren vo
     may hoac hien tren man hinh thiet bi khi vao che do provisioning) +
     provisioning_secret (chuoi bi mat rieng cua thiet bi, chi luu HASH
     trong DB o field provisioning_secret_hash, thiet bi tu giu ban goc
     trong firmware/EEPROM).
  2. App: nguoi dung quet QR hoac nhap tay device_code + provisioning_secret
     in kem thiet bi.
  3. BE: SELECT Device WHERE device_code=... AND status='provisioning'.
     - Khong tim thay -> 404 "Ma thiet bi khong ton tai hoac da duoc su
       dung".
     - Tim thay -> so sanh hash(secret_nhap_vao) voi provisioning_secret_hash.
       Sai -> 401 "Ma xac thuc thiet bi khong dung" (co the gioi han so
       lan thu qua LoginAttemptLog-style logic rieng neu can, nhung
       thuong gioi han o tang thiet bi/firmware roi vi day la 1 lan
       provisioning, khong phai dang nhap lap lai).
  4. Neu dung: UPDATE Device SET owner=user, status='online' (hoac
     'offline' cho toi khi thiet bi that su ket noi MQTT lan dau),
     updated_at=now (qua signal pre_save co san).
     Rang buoc CheckConstraint 'chk_devices_owner_vs_status' dam bao
     KHONG THE co Device status != provisioning ma owner rong, va
     nguoc lai -> tranh du lieu ma khong chu.
  5. Ghi AuditLog (action='DEVICE_CLAIMED', actor_user, device).
     Tao Notification chao mung + huong dan ket noi WiFi/Bluetooth.
  6. Ket qua: 200, tra ve thong tin Device vua duoc gan owner.

Loi: 404 sai ma, 401 sai secret, 409 thiet bi da co chu (status khong
con la 'provisioning' - da bi nguoi khac them truoc).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CHIA SE QUYEN DUNG KHOA (SHARE ACCESS CODE - 6 SO)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Nguoi tao (chu khoa hoac nguoi co quyen "share"):
  1. Chon Device + danh sach Permission muon cap (vi du: UNLOCK, VIEW_LOG).
  2. BE sinh ngau nhien ma 6 chu so (vi du: secrets.randbelow(900000)+100000).
  3. Tao ShareAccessCode: device, created_by, permissions (M2M),
     expires_at = now + SystemSettings.share_code_expiry_minutes,
     goi instance.set_code(ma_6_so) -> ma_hoa_bang_Fernet -> luu vao
     code_encrypted. KHONG luu plaintext o bat ky dau (khong log ra
     console/AuditLog.metadata).
  4. Tra ma 6 so ve cho nguoi tao (hien tren app 1 lan) de ho gui thu
     cong (nhan tin, doc mieng...) cho nguoi duoc chia se - KHONG gui
     qua he thong email/SMS cua app (giam be mat tan cong so voi OTP).
  5. Ket qua: 201, tra ve ma 6 so (chi lan nay) + thoi han het han.

Nguoi nhan nhap ma de kich hoat:
  1. Nhap device_code (hoac quet QR/link co san device_id) + ma 6 so.
  2. BE: SELECT ShareAccessCode WHERE device=... AND expires_at > now.
     Voi tung ban ghi con hieu luc cua thiet bi do, goi check_code(ma_nhap)
     (giai ma Fernet roi so sanh) cho toi khi khop hoac het danh sach.
     - Khong khop ban nao / khong con ban nao (het han/da bi xoa vi da
       dung) -> 400 "Ma khong hop le hoac da het han".
  3. Neu khop:
     - Tao DeviceAccess: device, user=nguoi_nhan, permissions=copy tu
       ShareAccessCode.permissions, source='SHARE_CODE', accepted=True,
       created_by=nguoi_tao_ma, is_active=True.
     - XOA NGAY ban ghi ShareAccessCode vua dung (DELETE, dung 1 lan).
     - Ghi AuditLog (action='SHARE_CODE_REDEEMED').
     - Tao Notification cho ca 2 ben (nguoi tao ma: "X da nhan quyen
       dung khoa"; nguoi nhan: "Ban da duoc cap quyen dung khoa Y").
  4. Ket qua: 200, tra ve DeviceAccess vua tao.

Don dep dinh ky (khong bat buoc nhung khuyen nghi): 1 scheduled job
(vi du Celery beat / cron) DELETE ShareAccessCode WHERE expires_at < now
- vi ma het han van con nam trong DB (o dang ma hoa) cho toi khi bi xoa
thu cong hoac bi job nay don.

Loi: 400 sai ma/het han, 404 device khong ton tai, 403 nguoi tao khong
co quyen "share" tren device do (kiem tra qua DeviceAccess/owner cua
nguoi tao truoc khi cho tao ShareAccessCode).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7. DUNG THE NFC DE MO KHOA (TAP-TO-UNLOCK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Luong nay khong qua app - xay ra ngay tai thiet bi:

  1. Nguoi dung ap the NFC vao dau doc gan tren khoa. Firmware doc UID
     vat ly cua the.
  2. Thiet bi (hoac gateway) tinh hash(UID) va goi len BE qua API/MQTT
     kem reader_id, device_id.
  3. BE: SELECT NfcReader WHERE id=reader_id AND device_id=device_id.
     Khong ton tai/da disable -> ghi NfcLog(event_type='TAP_FAILED',
     success=False), tra loi tu choi mo khoa.
  4. SELECT AccessCard WHERE card_uid_hash=hash(UID) AND is_active=True.
     - Khong tim thay the -> NfcLog TAP_FAILED (nfc_tag=None) -> tu choi.
     - Tim thay -> kiem tra CardDeviceAccess WHERE access_card=the AND
       device=device_id AND is_active=True.
       - Khong co quyen tren device nay -> NfcLog TAP_FAILED
         (nfc_tag=the, user=chu_the) -> tu choi, co the tao Notification
         canh bao cho chu device neu muon (the la vay nhung khong co quyen
         tren khoa nay - dau hieu bat thuong).
       - Co quyen -> tao NfcSession (reader, nfc_tag, device, user,
         session_token, success=True) -> gui DeviceCommand (command_type=
         'UNLOCK', issued_by=user, device) xuong thiet bi qua MQTT ->
         ghi NfcLog(event_type='TAP_SUCCESS', success=True) -> ghi
         DeviceStatusLog khi thiet bi bao cap nhat lock_state='unlocked'.
  5. Ket qua tra ve thiet bi: lenh mo khoa (rung/den bao xanh). Ket qua
     tren app (neu online): push Notification "Cua vua duoc mo bang the
     NFC luc HH:MM boi <ten user>".

Dang ky the moi (truoc khi dung duoc buoc tren):
  1. Chu khoa (hoac nguoi co quyen ADD_CARD) ap the trong che do
     "dang ky the" tren app/thiet bi.
  2. BE tao AccessCard (card_uid_hash=hash(UID), user=nguoi_so_huu_the).
  3. Tao CardDeviceAccess (access_card, device) de gan the vao (cac)
     khoa cu the - UniqueConstraint 'uniq_card_device' chan dang ky
     trung 1 the cho cung 1 device 2 lan.
  4. Ghi NfcLog(event_type='CARD_REGISTER'), AuditLog.

Loi thuong gap: 400 the da dang ky (unique card_uid_hash), 409 the da
gan cho device nay roi (uniq_card_device), 403 nguoi thao tac khong co
quyen ADD_CARD tren device.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
8. GHI CHU CHUNG VE BAO MAT
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tat ca "*_hash" (password, provisioning_secret, pin, card_uid, token,
  command_token, authorization_code, recovery_code) dung thuat toan bam
  1 chieu (vi du Argon2/bcrypt qua Django's password hasher hoac
  hashlib.sha256 cho token dai ngau nhien) - KHONG the giai nguoc.
- ShareAccessCode.code_encrypted la TRUONG HOP DUY NHAT dung ma hoa
  2 chieu (Fernet) thay vi hash, vi nghiep vu bat buoc phai so sanh
  ma 6 so ngan (de bi brute-force neu chi hash + so sanh tuan tu ma
  khong gioi han so lan/thoi gian) - da bu lai bang: doi song cuc ngan
  (mac dinh 15 phut), xoa ngay sau khi dung, va nen gioi han so lan
  thu redeem sai trong tang view (vi du qua cache/rate-limit theo
  device_id+ip, tuong tu logic LoginLockout).
- Khong con "2FA" trong toan bo he thong: 1 yeu to duy nhat de dang nhap
  la mat khau; rui ro do doi trong su ket hop: khoa dang nhap tang dan +
  email/token xac minh cho cac hanh dong nhay cam (doi mat khau, doi
  thong tin, thao tac ho tro) + audit log day du.
